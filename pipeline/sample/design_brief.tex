{\LARGE\textbf{Design brief}}\par\smallskip
{\textcolor{ink2}{Steam reviews of ELEX, a single-player open-world action role-playing game. Generated by the review pipeline on 1,200 reviews, 2026-11-10. Draft for review by the design team.}}

\medskip
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=head!45,colframe=head,boxrule=0pt,arc=2pt]
\textbf{In short.} To enhance player satisfaction, the game should first address combat dynamics \textbf{R1}, movement and evasion \textbf{R2}, and enemy strength and balance \textbf{R3} to ensure responsive and balanced gameplay. It is crucial to maintain the early game difficulty \textbf{A1} and overall difficulty \textbf{A2} as they contribute positively to player mastery and challenge. Additionally, the game should preserve elements like story engagement \textbf{P1}, world building \textbf{P2}, and factions and lore \textbf{P3} to maintain immersion and player engagement.
\end{tcolorbox}

\section*{Tensions to resolve}
{\small\textcolor{ink2}{A change that repairs one aspect may weaken another that players value. Each candidate pair in the same area of the game was checked; only direct conflicts are listed.}}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt]
  \item \textbf{Repair R3} (Enemy Strength and Balance) vs \textbf{Keep A1} (Early Game Difficulty)\\
If the team reduces enemy damage, then the early game difficulty may be weakened, affecting character development and progression. \textit{Watch:} Monitor player feedback on early game challenge and character growth in later reviews.
  \item \textbf{Repair R3} (Enemy Strength and Balance) vs \textbf{Keep A2} (Overall Difficulty and Mastery)\\
If the team reduces enemy damage, then the overall difficulty level may be perceived as lower, affecting player mastery and challenge. \textit{Watch:} Monitor player feedback on difficulty and challenge in later reviews.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Requirements}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white,colframe=gR,colbacktitle=lR,coltitle=black,title={\textbf{R1}\quad Combat Dynamics\hfill\textcolor{gR}{\textbf{Repair first}}},boxrule=0.8pt,arc=2pt,left=6pt,right=6pt,top=4pt,bottom=4pt,before skip=8pt,after skip=8pt]
{\large\textbf{The game must refine combat mechanics to ensure responsive and fluid interactions, reducing clunkiness and improving player satisfaction.}}

\smallskip
\textbf{Why.} Players experience combat as clunky, unpolished, and unenjoyable, detracting from overall game immersion.

\smallskip
{\small Mentioned in 536 of 1,200 reviews (45\%); 68\% of comments negative, 21\% positive. Of the players who criticize it, 53\% end up recommending the game (all players: 70\%). Reviews that mention it are less likely to be recommending the game {\color{ink2}\footnotesize(odds ratio 0.62, 95\% CI 0.48--0.79)}.}

\smallskip
\textbf{Players say.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textit{``the combat mechanics are clunky''}
    \item \textit{``The combat mechanics can feel clunky and unpolished at times''}
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Possible change.} Enhance combat responsiveness by optimizing input detection, balancing stamina usage, and refining enemy AI to provide a more fluid and engaging combat experience.

\smallskip
\textbf{Check after the change.} Post-change reviews should indicate improved combat fluidity, responsiveness, and enjoyment, with fewer mentions of clunkiness or frustration.

\medskip
{\small\textbf{Decision:}\quad $\square$ Accept\quad $\square$ Modify\quad $\square$ Reject\qquad Notes: \rule{0.38\linewidth}{0.4pt}}
\end{tcolorbox}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white,colframe=gR,colbacktitle=lR,coltitle=black,title={\textbf{R2}\quad Movement and Evasion\hfill\textcolor{gR}{\textbf{Repair first}}},boxrule=0.8pt,arc=2pt,left=6pt,right=6pt,top=4pt,bottom=4pt,before skip=8pt,after skip=8pt]
{\large\textbf{The game must improve movement responsiveness and reduce animation delays for actions like dodging and jetpacking.}}

\smallskip
\textbf{Why.} Players experience unresponsive controls and delayed animations, making evasion and movement frustrating.

\smallskip
{\small Mentioned in 163 of 1,200 reviews (14\%); 49\% of comments negative, 37\% positive. Of the players who criticize it, 33\% end up recommending the game (all players: 70\%). Reviews that mention it are less likely to be recommending the game {\color{ink2}\footnotesize(odds ratio 0.56, 95\% CI 0.40--0.79)}.}

\smallskip
\textbf{Players say.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textit{``The control responsiveness is terrible: saves that work intermittently, attacks that don't trigger on time, and elixirs disappearing due to enemies interrupting animations.''} {\footnotesize(translated from russian)}
    \item \textit{``You press space to fly with the backpack, but the character first completes all combat animations, then tries to fly.''} {\footnotesize(translated from russian)}
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Possible change.} Optimize control responsiveness and streamline animations for dodging, jetpacking, and other movement actions.

\smallskip
\textbf{Check after the change.} Reviews should indicate smoother movement and more responsive controls, with fewer complaints about animation delays.

\medskip
{\small\textbf{Decision:}\quad $\square$ Accept\quad $\square$ Modify\quad $\square$ Reject\qquad Notes: \rule{0.38\linewidth}{0.4pt}}
\end{tcolorbox}
\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=white,colframe=gR,colbacktitle=lR,coltitle=black,title={\textbf{R3}\quad Enemy Strength and Balance\hfill\textcolor{gR}{\textbf{Repair first}}},boxrule=0.8pt,arc=2pt,left=6pt,right=6pt,top=4pt,bottom=4pt,before skip=8pt,after skip=8pt]
{\large\textbf{The game must adjust enemy damage output to ensure player health is not depleted by a single hit from evenly-matched enemies.}}

\smallskip
\textbf{Why.} Players experience frustration from being one-hit KO'd by enemies, disrupting gameplay balance.

\smallskip
{\small Mentioned in 132 of 1,200 reviews (11\%); 89\% of comments negative, 6\% positive. Of the players who criticize it, 50\% end up recommending the game (all players: 70\%). Reviews that mention it are less likely to be recommending the game {\color{ink2}\footnotesize(odds ratio 0.47, 95\% CI 0.32--0.68)}.}

\smallskip
\textbf{Players say.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textit{``98\% of the enemies will 1 SHOT YOU''}
    \item \textit{``a single hit from any evenly-matched enemy will scythe down a huge chunk of your health''}
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Possible change.} Reduce the damage dealt by enemies to allow players to survive multiple hits, enhancing survivability and balance.

\smallskip
\textbf{Check after the change.} Reviews should indicate improved player survivability and balanced encounters, with fewer complaints about one-hit deaths.

\medskip
{\small\textbf{Decision:}\quad $\square$ Accept\quad $\square$ Modify\quad $\square$ Reject\qquad Notes: \rule{0.38\linewidth}{0.4pt}}
\end{tcolorbox}

    \item \textit{``It's not that stable of a game and not well optimized yet''}
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Possible change.} Enhance the game's engine to optimize resource management, reduce loading times, and improve frame rate stability.

\smallskip
\textbf{Check after the change.} Reviews should indicate smoother gameplay, reduced lag, and improved frame rates, with fewer technical issues.

\medskip
{\small\textbf{Decision:}\quad $\square$ Accept\quad $\square$ Modify\quad $\square$ Reject\qquad Notes: \rule{0.38\linewidth}{0.4pt}}

{\large\textbf{Preserve the sense of progression and engagement by ensuring that player skills and abilities continue to develop meaningfully over time.}}

\smallskip
\textbf{Why.} Players experience a rewarding journey as their skills grow, making previously challenging encounters easier.

\smallskip
{\small Mentioned in 69 of 1,200 reviews (6\%); 27\% of comments negative, 62\% positive. Of the players who criticize it, 58\% end up recommending the game (all players: 70\%). Reviews that mention it are more likely to be recommending the game {\color{ink2}\footnotesize(odds ratio 2.27, 95\% CI 1.19--4.33)}.}

\smallskip
\textbf{Players say.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=12pt,itemsep=1pt,topsep=2pt]
    \item \textit{``Previously impossible fights gradually become easier as you, and your skills, progress''}
    \item \textit{``After about 12h of suffering you will be able to finally enjoy the game by wrecking almost every NPC and visiting almost every place on the map since there are no restrictions except the final showdown''}
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Possible change.} Hypothesize that maintaining a balanced difficulty curve and skill progression will sustain player engagement.
